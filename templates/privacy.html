Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

← Back

1. Overview

VaultSwap is an invite-only Magic: The Gathering collection, pod trade, and buylist tracking application. This policy explains what data VaultSwap collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In Profile: When you sign in with Google OAuth 2.0, we receive and store your email address, Google account identifier (sub), display name, and profile avatar URL.
  • Optional Profile Info: You may optionally add a Discord handle so pod members can coordinate trades.
  • Collection & Binder Data: Card names, set codes, quantities, finishes, conditions, and trade status tags that you import or add manually.
  • Google Drive Sync Metadata: When you import a CSV from Google Drive, we record the selected file's name, Drive file ID, MD5 checksum, and the newest card created date from that file so future syncs only add newly created cards without duplicating or wiping your existing binder.

3. Google Drive Access (drive.file Scope)

VaultSwap requests the narrowest possible Google Drive permission (drive.file), which only grants access to the specific file(s) you explicitly select in the Google Picker.

  • VaultSwap never browses, lists, or accesses any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Your short-lived Google Drive OAuth access token is used only in memory during the import and is never saved to the database or disk.
  • VaultSwap's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Privacy by Default & Pod Sharing

All imported and added cards default to Private and are strictly scoped to your account. Other invited pod members can only see cards that you explicitly tag as Trade or Meeple in the shared Pod Feed, along with your display name and optional Discord handle.

5. Third-Party Services & Cookies

  • Scryfall & Mighty Meeple: Card identifiers are looked up against the Scryfall API for metadata/market pricing, and against Mighty Meeple only when you tag a card for trade or buylist. No personal user data is sent to these services.
  • Session Cookies: VaultSwap uses a signed HTTP session cookie (SameSite=Lax) strictly to keep you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and binder data only while you are an active member of the pod. You can delete individual cards from your binder at any time. To request full deletion of your account and all associated binder and Drive import records, please contact your pod administrator.
